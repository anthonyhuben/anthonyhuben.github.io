Privacy Policy

LicketySplit Downloader is built with a focus on speed and user privacy. We do not track your downloads, monitor your activity, or collect any personal information. The app only accesses the internet to fetch the files you've explicitly requested and to probe URLs for intelligent filename discovery.

What We Do Not Track

LicketySplit Downloader does not track your activity or collect information about the files you download or how you use the app.

Local Downloads

Your files are downloaded directly from the source servers to your local device. LicketySplit does not use intermediary servers to process your data or store your downloads remotely.

Smart Filename Discovery

When you provide a URL, LicketySplit intelligently probes the metadata to discover the correct filename. This process happens directly between your device and the target server, with no third-party tracking involved.

Sharing and Analytics

Because we do not track your information, there is nothing to sell, rent, or share for analytics or advertising purposes.
