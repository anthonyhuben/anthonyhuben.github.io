Memory Keeper Screen Recorder

Privacy Policy

Memory Keeper Screen Recorder is designed to keep captures local. The app does not collect analytics or sell your data, and it only uses the permissions needed to record the screen and optional system audio on your Mac.

Back to homepage Recording Storage Audio

What We Do Not Track

Memory Keeper does not track your activity, collect usage analytics, or build an advertising profile.

Screen Recording

The app captures the display, region, or window you choose using Apple's ScreenCaptureKit. Screen Recording permission is required for capture features, and the content stays on your Mac while you record.

System Audio

When you enable system audio, Memory Keeper captures it locally through ScreenCaptureKit without a separate loopback driver or third-party service. macOS may prompt for the permission required by that feature.

Local Storage

Recordings and screenshots are saved locally in your user Library/Application Support/MemoryKeeper/Recordings folder. The app also keeps a small local index so you can organize recordings with notes and tags. Nothing is uploaded by the app.

Sharing and Analytics

Because Memory Keeper does not collect your data, there is nothing to sell, rent, or share for analytics or advertising purposes.
