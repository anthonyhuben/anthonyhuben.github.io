Clean This Mac

Privacy Policy

Clean This Mac is built to keep cleanup work on your Mac. The app does not collect analytics, sell your data, or upload scan results to a server. It inspects the files and folders associated with the cleanup items you choose, then removes those files locally when you confirm the action.

What We Do Not Track

Clean This Mac does not track your activity, collect usage analytics, or build advertising profiles.

Local Scanning

When you run a scan, Clean This Mac checks local paths on your Mac to estimate how much space each cleanup item may free. That includes cache folders, logs, browser data, app support files, downloads, snapshots, and other macOS storage locations associated with the cleaners you select.

The app performs this work on-device. It does not upload your files, folder names, or scan results to external servers.

Cleaning and Deletion

When you confirm a clean, the app removes the selected local files and folders from your Mac using macOS file operations and built-in system tools where appropriate. This may include browser caches and history databases, temporary items, logs, downloads, snapshots, or app-specific cache data.

Because cleaning is destructive, the app asks you to confirm before permanently deleting selected items.

Files and Permissions

Clean This Mac is an app-sandboxed macOS utility. It works with the files and folders required for the cleanup tasks you choose, including locations in your Library, Caches, Logs, and Downloads folders, plus related application data when a cleaner targets it.

Depending on which items you select and how macOS is configured, the app may need permission to inspect or remove files in protected locations. Those permissions are used only to perform cleanup on your Mac.

Sharing and Analytics

Because Clean This Mac does not collect your data, there is nothing to sell, rent, or share for analytics or advertising purposes.
