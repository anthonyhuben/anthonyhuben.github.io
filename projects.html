Page Turner

Page Turner is a native macOS reader for books, scripts, scores, archives, media, and 3D assets. It keeps different formats readable in one place instead of scattering them across separate apps.

  • Local archive and page-set reading
  • Reader themes, fonts, and read-aloud
  • Media and 3D assets stay local

Seamless Clip

Seamless Clip is a macOS clip editor built around lossless trimming and a more faithful workflow for Matroska and WebM files.

  • Native Matroska/WebM handling
  • Stream-aware export and segment logs
  • Built for clean, local editing
