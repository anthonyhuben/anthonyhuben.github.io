Scrupulous

Privacy Policy

Scrupulous is designed to keep drafting local. The app does not collect analytics or send your writing to third-party services. Documents, previews, and exports stay on your Mac unless you choose to share them yourself.

Back to homepage Projects Documents Preview

What We Do Not Track

Scrupulous does not track your activity, collect analytics, or build advertising profiles.

Local Documents

Your Markdown and Fountain files stay on your Mac. The app reads and writes them locally through standard macOS file access instead of sending them to a server.

Preview and Export

Live preview, PDF export, and printing all happen locally. If you choose to export a file, Scrupulous writes it to the destination you select through the save panel.

Quick Reference

Built-in help and quick reference content stay inside the app. When future AI-assisted features are available, they are intended to stay on-device rather than sending drafts to a cloud service.

Sharing and Analytics

Because Scrupulous does not collect your data, there is nothing to sell, rent, or share for analytics or advertising purposes.

Updates to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will appear on this page.
