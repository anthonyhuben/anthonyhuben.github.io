Video Matrix

Privacy Policy

Video Matrix is designed to keep your work local. The app does not collect analytics, sell your data, or send your videos to a server. It reads the files you choose on your Mac to generate thumbnail contact sheets and saves exports only to the location you select.

What We Do Not Track

Video Matrix does not track your activity, collect usage analytics, or build advertising profiles.

Local Video Processing

When you open a video, Video Matrix processes that file locally on your Mac to extract frames, read video details, and build the contact sheet preview. The app does not upload the video, its frames, or its metadata to external servers.

Files You Choose

The app only accesses video files and save locations that you explicitly choose through the standard macOS open and save panels. It does not scan your files in the background or access unrelated folders without your action.

Exports and Storage

Exported PNG, JPEG, or HEIC files are written locally to the destination you select. Generated images, thumbnails, and project data stay on your device unless you decide to share them yourself.

Sharing and Analytics

Because Video Matrix does not collect your data, there is nothing to sell, rent, or share for analytics or advertising purposes.

Updates to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will appear on this page.
