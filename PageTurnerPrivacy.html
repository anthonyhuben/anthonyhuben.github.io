Privacy Policy

PageTurner Reader is designed to stay local. We do not track any information, all transcription is done on device and never goes online, and the web browser only accesses the Internet when you are using web pages in that mode.

What We Do Not Track

PageTurner Reader does not track your activity or collect information about how you use the app.

On-Device Transcription

All transcription is performed on your device and never goes online. Transcribed content is processed locally instead of being sent to servers.

Web Browser Mode

The built-in web browser only accesses the Internet when you intentionally open web pages in that mode. Reading and transcription features stay local unless you choose to browse the web.

Sharing and Analytics

Because we do not track your information, there is nothing to sell, rent, or share for analytics or advertising purposes.

Updates to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will appear on this page.
