FormatFlux

Privacy Policy

FormatFlux is designed to keep formatting work on your Mac. The app does not collect analytics, sell your data, or upload your files to a server. Formatting runs locally, and the documents you open stay on your device unless you choose to save, export, or share them.

What We Do Not Track

FormatFlux does not track your browsing activity, collect usage analytics, build advertising profiles, or send document content to remote services.

Local Formatting

When you format content, FormatFlux performs that work locally on your Mac. The app does not need to contact a server to apply formatting rules, preview results, or generate the final output.

Any formatting results remain on your device unless you copy them, save them, or export them yourself.

Files and Preferences

FormatFlux stores your preferences locally using macOS app storage. That may include things like recent choices, layout settings, or other customization you enable in the app.

If the app reads or writes files, those file operations happen on your Mac and only use the files you explicitly choose.

Clipboard, Copy, and Export

If you copy formatted output, it is placed on your Mac clipboard so you can paste it into another app or website. FormatFlux does not transmit clipboard contents to a server.

If you export content, the app writes the file to the location you choose. Those exports stay under your control on your device.

Sharing and Analytics

Because FormatFlux does not collect your data, there is nothing to sell, rent, or share for analytics or advertising purposes.
